## What Are Gaming Consoles?

Gaming consoles are systems that people use to play video games. Some of the most popular consoles are PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo. Each console is a little different, but they all have the same main purpose, which is to let people play games and have fun.

### Popular Consoles

PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo all have different things that make them popular.

- PlayStation
- Xbox
- Nintendo

PlayStation has games like Spider-Man and God of War. Xbox is known for games like Halo and its Game Pass service. Nintendo is different because it has games like Mario, Zelda, and Pokémon. The Nintendo Switch can also be used as a handheld system, which makes it different from the other consoles.

## Consoles and Controllers

Most gaming consoles use controllers to play games. Controllers have buttons, joysticks, and triggers that let players control their characters. [[Gaming Controllers]] are an important part of using a console because without one, most console games would be hard to play.

Gaming consoles can also be connected to a [[Gaming Monitors]] or a TV. People can also add different [[Gaming Accessories]] to make their setup better.

Gaming consoles have changed a lot over the years. Newer consoles have better graphics, faster loading, more storage, and more online features.
