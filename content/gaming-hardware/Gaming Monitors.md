## Why Gaming Monitors Matter

A gaming monitor is the screen that people use to see their games. The monitor can make a big difference in how a game looks and feels. There are many different monitors, so people can choose one based on what they want from their gaming setup.

### Monitor Features

Some of the main things people look at when buying a gaming monitor are resolution, refresh rate, screen size, and response time.

- Resolution
- Refresh rate
- Response time
- Screen size

Resolution affects how clear and detailed the picture looks. Refresh rate affects how smooth the game looks when things are moving on the screen. Response time is also important because it affects how quickly the monitor responds to what is happening.

## Monitors and Gaming PCs

Gaming monitors work closely with [[Gaming PCs]]. A gaming PC needs to be powerful enough to take advantage of a monitor's higher settings. For example, having a high refresh rate monitor does not help as much if the computer cannot run the game at a high frame rate.

Monitors can also be used with [[Gaming Consoles]], so they are not only for PC gamers.
