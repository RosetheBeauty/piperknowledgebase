# What Is Matchmaking?

Matchmaking is a system used in online multiplayer games to find and connect players for a game or match. It can help players find opponents or teammates based on things such as skill level, game mode, location, or connection quality.

Matchmaking can make it easier for players to join games without having to manually find other people to play with.

### Common Types of Matchmaking

Some common types of matchmaking include:

- Skill based matchmaking
- Ranked matchmaking
- Casual matchmaking
- Team matchmaking
- Quick matchmaking
- Cross platform matchmaking

Skill based matchmaking attempts to place players with others who have a similar level of skill. This can help create matches that are more balanced.

Ranked matchmaking is usually connected to a ranking system. Players compete against others while trying to improve their rank.

Casual matchmaking is designed for players who want to play without focusing as much on rankings or competitive progression.

Team matchmaking can create teams by placing players together before a match begins. Cross platform matchmaking allows players using different gaming systems to play together when a game supports it.

## Modern Examples

Some games that use matchmaking include:

- **Fortnite**: Uses matchmaking to place players into online matches and different game modes.
- **Call of Duty**: Uses matchmaking to create multiplayer matches based on different game settings and player information.
- **Rocket League**: Uses matchmaking to connect players for different competitive and casual game modes.
- **Overwatch 2**: Uses matchmaking to create teams for its different multiplayer modes.
- **VALORANT**: Uses matchmaking for both ranked and unranked multiplayer games.
- **League of Legends**: Uses matchmaking to create teams and matches for its different game modes.

### What Makes Matchmaking Different?

Matchmaking is different because it automatically helps players find other people to play with. Without matchmaking, players may have to search for teammates or opponents themselves.

Different games use different matchmaking systems. Some focus more on skill, while others may consider connection quality, location, party size, or the selected game mode.

Matchmaking can also take different amounts of time. A game may find a match quickly when many people are playing, while less populated game modes may take longer.

### What I Would Use

I would use matchmaking when I want to quickly join a game without having to find players myself. It is especially useful when I want to play multiplayer games but do not already have a group ready.
