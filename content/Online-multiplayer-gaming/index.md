# What Is Online & Multiplayer Gaming?

Online and multiplayer gaming allows players to connect with other people through video games. Players can compete against each other, work together, or simply explore a game with friends. Some multiplayer games require an internet connection, while others allow players to play together on the same device or network.

Online gaming has become a major part of gaming because players can connect with people from different places. Multiplayer games can include small groups of friends or large communities with thousands of players.

### Common Types of Online & Multiplayer Gaming

Some common types of online and multiplayer gaming include:

- Cooperative games
- Competitive games
- Online role playing games
- Battle royale games
- Multiplayer shooters
- Sports games
- Racing games
- Massively multiplayer online games

Cooperative games allow players to work together toward a shared goal. Players may complete missions, defeat enemies, or solve problems as a team.

Competitive games have players or teams competing against each other. These games can include ranked modes, tournaments, and other competitive features.

Battle royale games place multiple players or teams into the same match, with players competing until one player or team remains.

Massively multiplayer online games allow large numbers of players to interact in the same game world. These games can include quests, social activities, trading, and other online features.

## Modern Examples

Some popular online and multiplayer games include:

- **Fortnite**: An online game that includes battle royale, building, and other multiplayer modes.
- **Call of Duty: Warzone**: A multiplayer shooter with large online matches.
- **Minecraft**: A sandbox game that allows players to build, explore, and play together online.
- **Grand Theft Auto Online**: An online open world where players can complete activities, missions, and other activities with other players.
- **Rocket League**: A competitive sports game that combines soccer with cars.
- **Baldur's Gate 3**: A role playing game that allows players to experience the story together through multiplayer.

### What Makes Online & Multiplayer Gaming Different?

Online and multiplayer gaming is different because it allows players to interact with other people while playing. Players can communicate, cooperate, compete, and build friendships through games.

Online games can also change over time. Developers may add new content, events, maps, characters, and other features to keep players interested.

A stable internet connection can also be important for online gaming. Connection problems can cause lag, delays, or players being disconnected from a game.
