# What Is Team Based Gaming?

Team based gaming is a type of multiplayer gaming where players work together as a team to reach a shared goal. Instead of playing completely on their own, players have to communicate, plan, and help their teammates. Different games can give each player a different role or responsibility.

### Common Types of Team Based Gaming

- Team shooters
- Team sports games
- Cooperative games
- Strategy games
- Multiplayer role playing games
- Objective based games
- Team battle games

Team shooters are games where players work together to defeat the other team. Players may have different weapons, abilities, or roles that help their team.

Team sports games allow players to work together just like they would in a real sport. Players may control one character while their teammates control other players on the team.

Cooperative games allow players to work together against computer controlled enemies or other challenges. The goal is usually to complete missions or objectives together.

Strategy games can require players to work together by sharing resources, planning attacks, or controlling different parts of a match.

Multiplayer role playing games can allow players to form groups and complete quests, defeat enemies, or explore the game world together.

Objective based games give teams specific goals to complete. These goals can include capturing an area, protecting an object, or delivering something to a specific location.

Team battle games focus on two or more teams competing against each other. Players usually need to work together and use different abilities or roles to win.

## Modern Examples

- Overwatch 2
- Valorant
- League of Legends
- Fortnite
- Rocket League
- Minecraft

Overwatch 2 is a team based shooter where players choose different heroes and work together to complete objectives.

Valorant uses teams where players have different abilities and roles that can help the team during a match.

League of Legends is a team based strategy game where players work together to defeat the opposing team and destroy its base.

Fortnite includes team based modes where players can work together to survive, complete objectives, and defeat other players.

Rocket League allows players to work together to score goals against another team.

Minecraft allows players to work together by building, exploring, collecting resources, and completing activities in shared worlds.

### What Makes Team Based Gaming Different?

Team based gaming is different because players have to think about more than their own actions. They also have to pay attention to what their teammates are doing. Communication and teamwork can be important because one player's actions can affect the entire team.

### What I Would Play

I would probably play team based games with friends because I like being able to work toward the same goal. I also think having different roles makes multiplayer games more interesting because everyone can contribute in a different way.

> I like team based games because everyone can have a role and work together to reach the same goal.
