# What Is Xbox?

Xbox is a gaming platform created by Microsoft. It includes gaming consoles, video games, online services, and gaming accessories. Xbox has been a major part of console gaming since the original Xbox was released in 2001.

### Common Xbox Systems

Some of the main Xbox systems include:

- Xbox
- Xbox 360
- Xbox One
- Xbox Series X
- Xbox Series S
- Xbox Cloud Gaming

The Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S are Microsoft's current gaming consoles. The Series X is designed for higher performance and can play games using physical discs, while the Series S is a smaller digital only console.

Xbox also offers services that allow players to access games across different devices. Xbox Game Pass gives players access to a large library of games through different membership options.

### Common Xbox Features

Xbox includes several features that players can use while gaming:

- Online multiplayer
- Xbox Game Pass
- Cloud gaming
- Digital game downloads
- Backward compatibility
- Xbox controllers

Online multiplayer allows players to connect with friends and other players. Xbox Game Pass gives players access to a large selection of games that can be downloaded or played through supported services.

Backward compatibility also allows certain older Xbox games to be played on newer Xbox systems.

## Modern Examples

Some popular Xbox games and services include:

- **Xbox Series X**: Microsoft's high performance current generation gaming console.
- **Xbox Series S**: A smaller digital only Xbox console.
- **Xbox Game Pass**: A subscription service that gives players access to a large library of games.
- **Forza Horizon 5**: An open world racing game where players can drive different cars and explore Mexico.
- **Halo Infinite**: A first person shooter featuring the character Master Chief.
- **Minecraft**: A sandbox game where players can explore, build, craft, and survive.

### What Makes Xbox Different?

Xbox is different because Microsoft connects its console, PC, and cloud gaming services. This gives players different ways to access and play Xbox games.

Xbox also focuses heavily on online gaming and services such as Game Pass. Players can use Xbox controllers and other accessories with supported devices.
