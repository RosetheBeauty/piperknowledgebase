# What Is Nintendo?

Nintendo is a video game company known for creating gaming systems and popular video game series. The company has been making video games for many years and has created characters such as Mario, Link, Kirby, and Pikachu. Nintendo is also known for making games that are designed around its own gaming systems.

### Common Nintendo Platforms

Some of Nintendo's main gaming platforms include:

- Nintendo Switch
- Nintendo Switch 2
- Nintendo 3DS
- Wii U
- Wii
- Nintendo DS

The Nintendo Switch became popular because it can be used as both a home console and a handheld gaming system. Players can connect it to a television or use the system as a portable device.

The Nintendo Switch 2 is Nintendo's newer gaming system. It continues the idea of having a system that can be used at home or taken on the go.

Nintendo has also created handheld systems such as the Nintendo DS and Nintendo 3DS. These systems allowed players to play games using a portable device with features such as two screens and touchscreen controls.

## Modern Examples

Some popular Nintendo games and systems include:

- **Nintendo Switch 2**: Nintendo's current gaming system that can be used in handheld mode or connected to a television.
- **Super Mario Bros. Wonder**: A platforming game featuring Mario and other characters as they travel through colorful levels.
- **The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom**: An adventure game where players explore a large world, solve puzzles, and complete quests.
- **Mario Kart 8 Deluxe**: A racing game where players race against each other using characters and items from the Mario series.
- **Animal Crossing: New Horizons**: A life simulation game where players build and customize their own island.
- **Pokémon Legends: Z-A**: A Pokémon role playing game set in Lumiose City.

### What Makes Nintendo Different?

Nintendo is different because the company creates both gaming hardware and many of the games played on its systems. Nintendo also has several game series that are strongly connected to its platforms.

Nintendo games often focus on colorful worlds, unique characters, and gameplay that can be enjoyed by different types of players. The company also creates systems that can offer different ways to play.

### What I Would Play

I would probably play Nintendo games when I want something more relaxed or something I can play with other people. Games like Mario Kart and Animal Crossing are also good examples of games that can be played without needing a serious competitive setup.

> I think Nintendo is fun because it has a lot of games that are easy to pick up but can still be really entertaining.
