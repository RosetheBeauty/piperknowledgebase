# What Is PC Gaming?

PC gaming is playing video games on a personal computer. Unlike gaming consoles, PCs can be built and upgraded with different parts and accessories. PC gaming also gives players access to a large selection of games from different stores and platforms.

### Common Parts of PC Gaming

Some common parts of PC gaming include:

- Gaming computers
- Gaming keyboards
- Gaming mice
- Gaming monitors
- Gaming headsets
- Gaming controllers
- PC game stores
- Online multiplayer games

Gaming computers can come in different sizes and levels of performance. Some players build their own PCs, while others buy a computer that is already built.

Gaming keyboards and mice are commonly used for PC games. A keyboard can be used for movement and other controls, while a mouse can help with aiming and navigating menus.

Gaming monitors can also be important for PC gaming. Different monitors can have different screen sizes, resolutions, and refresh rates.

## Modern Examples

Some popular parts and platforms used for PC gaming include:

- **Gaming PCs**: Computers designed to run video games with stronger processors, graphics cards, and other hardware.
- **Steam**: A digital gaming platform where players can buy, download, and play PC games.
- **Epic Games Store**: A digital store that offers PC games and free game promotions.
- **Gaming Keyboards**: Keyboards designed with features that can make gaming more comfortable.
- **Gaming Mice**: Mice designed for gaming with features such as adjustable sensitivity and extra buttons.
- **Gaming Monitors**: Monitors made for gaming that can offer higher refresh rates and resolutions.
### What Makes PC Gaming Different?

PC gaming is different because players have more choices when it comes to their hardware and setup. Players can choose different computer parts, monitors, keyboards, mice, and other accessories.

PC games can also have different settings that allow players to adjust graphics, controls, and other options. Some games can also support mods, which allow players to change or add content to a game.
