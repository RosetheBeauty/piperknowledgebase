# What Are Gaming Platforms?

Gaming platforms are the systems and devices people use to play video games. A platform can be a gaming console, computer, mobile device, or handheld system. Different platforms can have their own games, features, controllers, and ways to play.

### Common Types of Gaming Platforms

Some common gaming platforms include:

- Gaming consoles
- Gaming PCs
- Mobile devices
- Handheld gaming systems
- Online gaming platforms
- Cloud gaming services

Gaming consoles are systems made for playing games on a television or monitor. PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo are some of the most well known gaming platforms.

PC gaming allows players to play games on computers. PC players can also choose different hardware and accessories to create a setup that fits their needs.

Mobile gaming allows people to play games on smartphones and tablets. These games can range from simple mobile games to larger games that require more powerful devices.

Handheld gaming systems allow players to play games while away from a television or desktop computer. Some handheld systems can also connect to a television.

Cloud gaming allows players to stream games through the internet instead of running the game directly on their own device.

## Modern Examples

Some popular gaming platforms include:

- **PlayStation**: A gaming platform from Sony that includes consoles, games, online services, and accessories.
- **Xbox**: Microsoft's gaming platform that includes consoles, games, online services, and PC gaming.
- **Nintendo**: A gaming company known for systems such as the Nintendo Switch and games featuring characters like Mario and Link.
- **PC Gaming**: A platform that allows players to play games on computers using different hardware and gaming services.
- **Mobile Gaming**: Gaming on smartphones and tablets through platforms such as iOS and Android.

### What Makes Gaming Platforms Different?

Gaming platforms can have different game libraries, hardware, controllers, online features, and prices. Some games may only be available on certain platforms, while others can be played across multiple systems.

The platform someone chooses can also depend on how they like to play. Someone who enjoys playing on a television may prefer a console, while someone who wants more customization may prefer a PC. People who want to play games while traveling may prefer mobile or handheld gaming.

### What I Would Use

If I was choosing a gaming platform, I would want something that gives me access to the games I actually want to play. I would also consider how comfortable the system is to use and whether I could play with friends.

> I think the best gaming platform depends on the games you like and how you want to play them.
