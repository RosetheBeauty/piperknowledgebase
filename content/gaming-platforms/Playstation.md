# What Is PlayStation?

PlayStation is a gaming platform created by Sony. It includes gaming consoles, video games, online services, and accessories. PlayStation has been a major part of console gaming since the original PlayStation was released in 1994.

### Common PlayStation Systems

Some of the main PlayStation systems include:

- PlayStation
- PlayStation 2
- PlayStation 3
- PlayStation 4
- PlayStation 5
- PlayStation Portal


The PlayStation 5 is Sony's current home gaming console. It allows players to play games digitally or with physical game discs. The system also supports online gaming and other entertainment features.

PlayStation consoles have also had different controllers over the years. The DualSense controller for the PlayStation 5 includes features such as adaptive triggers and haptic feedback.

## Modern Examples

Some popular PlayStation games and systems include:

- **PlayStation 5**: Sony's current home gaming console.
- **PlayStation Portal**: A handheld device that allows players to remotely play compatible games from their PlayStation 5.
- **Marvel's Spider-Man 2**: An action adventure game featuring Peter Parker and Miles Morales.
- **God of War Ragnarök**: An action adventure game that follows Kratos and Atreus.
- **The Last of Us Part II**: A story focused action adventure game.
- **Astro Bot**: A platforming game designed around exploration and creative gameplay.

### What Makes PlayStation Different?

PlayStation is known for its large selection of games and its exclusive game series. The platform also offers online services and different accessories that can change how players experience their games.

The PlayStation 5 also uses features such as fast loading and the DualSense controller's haptic feedback and adaptive triggers.
