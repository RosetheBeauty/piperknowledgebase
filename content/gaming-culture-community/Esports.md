## Esports

## What Is Esports?

Esports is a form of competitive gaming where players or teams compete against each other in organized video game competitions. Esports can include professional players, teams, tournaments, and large events. Some competitions are played online, while others are held in front of live audiences.

### Common Types of Esports

Some common types of esports include:

* First person shooter games
* Multiplayer online battle arena games
* Fighting games
* Sports games
* Strategy games
* Racing games

First person shooter games are popular in esports because teams can compete against each other in organized matches. Multiplayer online battle arena games usually have teams that work together to defeat the other team.

Fighting games usually have players compete against each other one on one. Sports games can also be used for esports competitions where players compete in games based on sports like football or basketball.

Strategy games require players to plan their moves and make decisions during a competition. Racing games allow players to compete against each other by racing cars or other vehicles.

## Modern Examples

Some popular esports games include:

* **League of Legends**: A multiplayer online battle arena game where teams compete against each other.
* **Counter-Strike 2**: A first person shooter where teams compete in organized matches.
* **VALORANT**: A tactical shooter where teams use different characters and abilities to compete.
* **Overwatch 2**: A team based shooter where players choose different heroes with unique abilities.
* **Street Fighter 6**: A fighting game where players compete against each other in one on one matches.
* **Rocket League**: A competitive game that combines soccer with cars.

### What Makes Esports Different?

Esports are different from regular gaming because the main focus is organized competition. Players can practice for hours, join teams, participate in tournaments, and compete for prizes.

Esports can also bring people together through online communities and live events. Fans can watch professional players compete, talk about matches, and support their favorite teams and players.

### What I Would Watch

If I was going to watch an esports competition, I would probably choose a game that I already enjoy playing or understand. It would be more interesting to watch when I know what the players are trying to accomplish.

> I think esports are interesting because they turn games that people normally play for fun into organized competitions.
