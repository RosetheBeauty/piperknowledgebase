## Role Playing Games (RPGs)

## What Are RPGs?

RPG stands for role-playing game. RPGs are a type of video game where players control a character or group of characters and make progress by completing quests, gaining experience, finding equipment, and developing their characters. Many RPGs also focus on story, exploration, and choices.

### Common Types of RPGs

Some common types of RPGs include:

- Action RPGs
- Turn-based RPGs
- MMORPGs
- Tactical RPGs
- Open-world RPGs

Action RPGs allow players to control their characters in real time, while turn-based RPGs usually have players take turns during battles. MMORPGs allow many players to play together in a large online world. Tactical RPGs focus more on strategy and positioning during battles.

## Modern Examples

There are many popular RPGs that have been released in recent years. Some examples include:

- **Baldur's Gate 3** – An RPG that focuses on character choices, exploration, quests, combat, and a large story.
- **Elden Ring** – An action RPG that combines real-time combat with exploration, character customization, equipment, and challenging enemies.
- **Final Fantasy VII Rebirth** – An RPG that combines real-time combat, exploration, characters, quests, and a large story.
- **Dragon Age: The Veilguard** – An RPG that focuses on story, character relationships, exploration, quests, and combat.
- **Metaphor: ReFantazio** – A role-playing game that combines turn-based combat, character development, exploration, and storytelling.
- **Monster Hunter Wilds** – An action RPG focused on hunting large monsters, creating equipment, exploring environments, and improving your character.

These games show how different RPGs can be. Some focus heavily on story and character choices, while others focus more on combat, exploration, strategy, or playing with other people.

### What Makes RPGs Different?

One thing that makes RPGs different is character progression. Players can usually make their characters stronger by gaining experience, learning abilities, finding better equipment, or completing quests.

### What I Would Play

If I was choosing an RPG, I would probably want one with a good story, character customization, and a lot to explore. I like when my choices can affect how I play the game or how the story develops.
