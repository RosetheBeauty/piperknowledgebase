## Adventure Games

## What Are Adventure Games?

Adventure games are a type of video game that focuses on exploration, story, puzzles, and discovering new places. Players usually control a character and explore the game world while completing objectives and making progress through the story. Adventure games can also include action, combat, or other gameplay features.

### Common Types of Adventure Games

Some common types of adventure games include:

- Story-based adventure games
- Action-adventure games
- Point-and-click adventure games
- Open-world adventure games
- Narrative adventure games

Story-based adventure games focus heavily on the characters and plot. Point-and-click games usually involve exploring areas, talking to characters, and solving puzzles. Open-world adventure games give players larger areas to explore and often allow them to choose what they want to do.

## Modern Examples

There are many modern games that include adventure elements. Some examples include:

- **The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom** – An adventure game that focuses on exploration, puzzles, combat, and discovering the world of Hyrule.
- **Indiana Jones and the Great Circle** – An adventure game that combines exploration, puzzles, combat, and a story based on Indiana Jones.
- **Star Wars Outlaws** – An open-world adventure game that allows players to explore different planets while completing missions and interacting with characters.
- **Life Is Strange: Double Exposure** – A story-focused adventure game that centers on characters, choices, exploration, and solving mysteries.
- **Assassin's Creed Shadows** – An action-adventure game that combines exploration, combat, stealth, and story elements.
- **The Plucky Squire** – An adventure game that combines exploration, puzzles, platforming, and a story that moves between a storybook and a 3D world.

These games show that adventure games can have very different styles. Some focus more on exploration and puzzles, while others focus on storytelling, combat, or open-world exploration.

### What Makes Adventure Games Different?

One thing that makes adventure games different is the focus on discovering things and moving through a story. Players may have to explore an area, find an important item, solve a puzzle, or talk to another character to continue.

Adventure games can also be combined with other genres. For example, action-adventure games include combat and faster gameplay, while open-world adventure games give players more freedom to explore.

### What I Would Play

If I was choosing an adventure game, I would probably want one with a good story and a world that gives me a lot to explore. I like games where I can take my time looking around instead of only following one objective.

> I think adventure games are fun because they let you explore a world while also giving you a story to follow.
