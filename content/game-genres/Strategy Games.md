## Strategy Games

## What Are Strategy Games?

Strategy games are games that focus on planning, decision making, and thinking ahead. Players usually have to make choices that can affect what happens later in the game. Some strategy games can be played slowly, while others require players to make quick decisions.

### Common Types of Strategy Games

Some common types of strategy games include:

* Real time strategy games
* Turn based strategy games
* 4X strategy games
* Tactical strategy games
* Tower defense games
* Card strategy games

Real time strategy games let players make decisions while the game is happening. Players may need to build bases, collect resources, and control units.

Turn based strategy games give players time to think before making their next move. Each player takes a turn before the game moves forward.

4X strategy games usually involve exploring, expanding, managing resources, and competing with other players or groups. These games can have large maps and can take a long time to complete.

Tactical strategy games focus more on individual battles and careful positioning. Players have to think about where their characters or units should move and how they should attack.

## Modern Examples

Some modern strategy games include:

* **Civilization VII**: A turn based strategy game where players build and manage a civilization.
* **Age of Empires IV**: A real time strategy game where players build civilizations, collect resources, and fight other players.
* **Manor Lords**: A strategy and city building game where players manage a medieval settlement.
* **Frostpunk 2**: A city building and survival strategy game where players make difficult decisions to keep their city running.
* **Total War: Warhammer III**: A strategy game that combines large battles with managing an empire.
* **Balatro**: A card based game that mixes poker ideas with strategy and deck building.

### What Makes Strategy Games Different?

Strategy games are different because winning is usually based on the choices a player makes instead of just how quickly they can react. Players may have to manage resources, plan attacks, build their team, or decide what they should do next.

Some strategy games can also have many different ways to play. A player might focus on building a strong economy, defending their area, attacking other players, or completing certain goals.

### What I Would Play

If I was going to play a strategy game, I would probably choose one that lets me take my time and plan my moves. I like games where my choices actually matter and can change what happens later in the game.

> I think strategy games are fun because you have to actually think about what you are doing instead of just rushing through the game.
